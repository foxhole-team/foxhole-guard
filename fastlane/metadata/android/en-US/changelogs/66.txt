Native FoxCore runtime:
• VPN and Tor now run only through the Rust core, with no fallback engine;
• strict typed configs cover VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks/Outline, Hysteria2, WireGuard, Naive, TUIC and AnyTLS;
• one owner controls TUN start, reload, network changes and teardown;
• DNS filtering statistics now come from typed core counters and events;
• Android UI, CI and release ABI checks are wired directly to FoxCore.
