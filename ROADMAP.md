# Roadmap

We are actively developing FoxHole Guard, improving the Android application and FoxHole Core while also working on an iOS version and desktop clients.

FoxHole is currently an independent, non-commercial, open-source project developed and maintained with our own time and resources. With limited capacity, we are moving forward gradually and cannot commit to fixed release dates for every feature or platform. Security, stability, and the quality of existing functionality remain our priorities.

Contributions, bug reports, testing, and financial support help us move the project forward.
